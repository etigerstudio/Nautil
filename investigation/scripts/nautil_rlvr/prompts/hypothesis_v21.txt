You check which of an investigator's hypotheses a batch of newly returned evidence bears on. The investigator keeps a numbered set of hypotheses (H1, H2, ...) and, after each batch of evidence, writes a ledger line per hypothesis ("H1 [old status → new status] — reason"). You will see, in order, every update of one investigation. For each update you get: the hypothesis state BEFORE the update, the evidence items JUST RETURNED (full text; possibly none), the investigator's update, and the list of hypothesis IDs to assess.

You do not grade the ledger itself (a program compares your answers with the ledger). For EVERY update you answer, with one-sentence reasons:

Per hypothesis (every ID in "Hypotheses to assess", in that order):
 - touched: does at least one item of THIS batch DIRECTLY BEAR on this hypothesis? yes / no.
   DIRECTLY BEARS: an item reports an observation, measurement, test result, examination finding or first-hand statement about the specific event, component, condition, person or mechanism that the hypothesis names, so that it makes the hypothesis more or less likely. Example: a witness saying the engine "sounded like it was missing" and then went silent directly bears on "loss of engine power". Background, administrative or general material (registration, generic procedures, operator history, weather when the hypothesis does not concern weather) does not directly bear. If nothing new was returned, every hypothesis is "no".
 - direction: when touched = yes, does the evidence, taken together, point towards the hypothesis ("supports") or against it ("weakens")? Evidence that bears directly but is not conclusive still points one way; choose the way it points. When touched = no, direction must be "unrelated". (touched = yes with "unrelated", or touched = no with "supports"/"weakens", is invalid.)
Judge from the state before and the evidence just returned only, not from the investigator's own opinion.

main: the ID of the hypothesis the batch bears on MOST directly (must be one with touched = yes); null when no hypothesis is touched.

H5. Is the reason written for the ledger changes, or for keeping statuses unchanged, grounded in THIS batch's evidence: does it cite at least one of the items just returned (by ID or by clearly identifiable content) and describe that item's content accurately? yes / no. (If nothing new was returned, answer yes when the investigator correctly says that no new evidence arrived, otherwise no.)

Answer with JSON only, no prose outside JSON, exactly this shape, one entry per update in the given order (the "updates" array must have exactly as many entries as there are updates, numbered 1, 2, ...; each "hypotheses" array lists exactly the IDs given for that update, in that order).
{"updates": [{"update": 1,
              "hypotheses": [{"id": "H1", "why": "<one sentence>", "touched": "yes|no", "direction": "supports|weakens|unrelated"}, ...],
              "main": "<hypothesis id or null>",
              "H5": {"why": "<one sentence>", "answer": "yes|no"}}, ...]}
