You check the final answer of an investigator who worked a case file by fetching evidence items. The final answer ends with CASE CLOSED (the cause is established) or CASE NOT CLOSED (the evidence does not settle it; the answer then gives its best tentative explanation and states the gap: what remains unresolved and why).

You get: the task question; the investigator's decision; the final answer; the investigator's last hypothesis state; the FULL TEXT of every evidence item cited in the final answer (flagged if it was never read or does not exist); the titles of all items the investigator READ and of all items it did NOT READ; and a REFERENCE that was never shown to the investigator: the reference closure decision, a reference final answer by an expert investigator on the same file, the official conclusion when one exists, and alternatives the official record left open. Whether the decision (closed / not closed) matches the reference is scored separately by a program: do NOT answer any question below merely from the decision marker; judge the content.

You do not give a score. You answer a checklist, each answer with a one-sentence reason. For CASE NOT CLOSED, "the investigator's cause" means its tentative leading explanation (if it names none, what it says is most likely). When the reference itself is not closed, compare with the reference's leading explanation and the alternatives it keeps open.

CONCLUSION checklist (always):
C1a. Is the INITIATING EVENT or TRIGGER the same as the reference's (the first thing that went wrong, e.g. "loss of engine power", "the pilot turned at the wrong beacon", "a memory leak in service X")? yes / no.
C1b. Is the CAUSAL CHAIN the same as the reference's (how the trigger led to the outcome, and the main contributing factors)? yes = same chain, even if worded differently or less detailed; partial = some links right but a key link missing, vaguer or wrong; no = a different or contradicting chain, or no chain at all while the reference gives one.
C2. Is the strength of the conclusion within what the evidence supports (no overreach)? yes = every claim is stated no more strongly than the evidence the investigator read supports (things the evidence only suggests are called likely, not established). no = at least one claim is stated as established although the read evidence only suggests it, or goes beyond it.
C3. List every CAUSAL CLAIM in the final answer (a statement that something caused, led to, contributed to, or ruled out something else; at most 12, merge near-duplicates), and for each say whether it is SUPPORTED: backed by a citation to an item the investigator read whose text actually contains that support. A claim with no citation, citing an item flagged as never read or non-existent, or whose cited text does not say what is claimed, is NOT supported. If the answer makes no causal claim, give an empty list.
C4. Does the answer address the main counter-evidence or competing explanations (explaining why they are discounted, or keeping them open)? yes / no. (no = it ignores an alternative that the evidence it read, or the reference, makes clearly relevant.)

GAP checklist (only when the decision is CASE NOT CLOSED; when CASE CLOSED, "gap" must be null):
G1. Does the gap name the specific missing evidence or check (e.g. "no teardown of the fuel pump was performed", "the maintenance record for the valve is not in the file") rather than only saying the cause is uncertain? yes / no.
G2. Only when G1 = yes: is the evidence the gap says is missing GENUINELY ABSENT from the case file? GENUINELY ABSENT = nothing among the READ or NOT READ titles could supply it. yes = absent; no = a NOT READ title (or a READ item) plausibly contains it, i.e. the gap is really the investigator's own omission. When G1 = no, answer "n/a" (and only then).
G3. Is this gap the reason the reference considers the case unsettled? yes = it matches what the reference (reference answer, official conclusion, open alternatives) says remains undetermined and why. no = a different reason, or the reference considers the case settled (reference decision CASE CLOSED).
